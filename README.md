# 🚀 Synapse Ledger
> **A Blockchain-Enabled IoT Trust Infrastructure for Secure Logistics**

## 🌍 Overview
Synapse Ledger is not just a project — it’s a foundational blueprint for the next generation of supply chain management. Built at the intersection of **IoT, Blockchain, and Edge Systems**, Synapse Ledger transforms physical logistics assets into verifiable, intelligent, and autonomous trust nodes. 

We bridge the gap between physical world events (locks, sensors, tampering) and digital trust (blockchain verification, immutable logs). In our ecosystem, every action is authorized, recorded, and mathematically provable.

*"If you cannot verify it, you cannot trust it."*
